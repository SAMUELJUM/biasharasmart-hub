Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

BiasharaSmart Hub uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage, and secure our platform.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences and understand how you interact with our service.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Required for authentication, security, and basic functionality. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand user behavior (e.g., pages visited, time spent). We use this data to improve our platform.

Preference Cookies: Remember your settings like language and dashboard layout.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted partners (e.g., Google Analytics) that set their own cookies. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may break certain features like login and M-Pesa integration.
