Data Protection

Compliant with Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019)

Effective date: April 1, 2026

BiasharaSmart Hub is fully committed to protecting the personal and business data of our users. This document outlines our compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Our Data Protection Principles

  • Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency in data processing
  • Purpose limitation — data collected only for specified, explicit purposes
  • Data minimization — we only collect what is strictly necessary
  • Accuracy — we maintain mechanisms to keep data correct and up-to-date
  • Storage limitation — data is retained only as long as needed
  • Integrity and confidentiality — strong security measures including encryption

Data Subject Rights

Under Kenyan law, you have the right to: access your data, request rectification, request erasure (right to be forgotten), restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing. To exercise these rights, email dpo@biasharasmarthub.co.ke.

Data Protection Officer

Our DPO oversees compliance and can be contacted at: Westlands Business Park, Nairobi, or dpo@biasharasmarthub.co.ke.

Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) within 72 hours as required by law.

International Data Transfers

All business data is stored on servers located in Kenya. We do not transfer personal data outside Kenya without your explicit consent and adequate safeguards.

Children's Data

Our platform is not intended for persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Complaints

If you believe we have violated your data protection rights, you may lodge a complaint with the ODPC (www.odpc.go.ke) or contact us directly.
