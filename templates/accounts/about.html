About BiasharaSmart Hub

Empowering Kenyan SMEs with AI-driven business intelligence

BiasharaSmart Hub was born from a simple observation: Kenyan small and medium enterprises are the backbone of our economy, yet most lack access to affordable, intelligent business tools. We set out to change that.

Our Mission

To democratize business intelligence for every Kenyan entrepreneur. We believe that running a business shouldn't mean guessing — every SME deserves real-time insights, accurate forecasts, and easy access to their data, regardless of technical skill or internet connectivity.

🇰🇪

Kenya First

Built specifically for Kenyan SMEs with M-Pesa integration, USSD access, and local support.

🤖

AI for Good

Machine learning models that help business owners make smarter decisions, not replace them.

🌱

Sustainable Growth

Affordable pricing ensures that growth isn't reserved for large corporations.

Our Story

Founded in 2026 by a team of MMUST developers, data scientists, and business advisors, BiasharaSmart Hub launched after 18 months of research alongside 200+ small business owners across Nairobi, Kisumu, Mombasa, and rural regions. We learned that the biggest challenges aren't just about bookkeeping — they're about visibility, forecasting, and access to credit. Our platform addresses all three.

Vision 2030 Alignment

BiasharaSmart Hub directly supports Kenya's Vision 2030 economic pillar by digitizing SMEs, improving financial inclusion, and creating data-driven entrepreneurship. We're proud to be part of Kenya's digital transformation journey.
